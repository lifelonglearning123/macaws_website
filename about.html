Our purpose

We help businesses work smarter — not harder

At Macaws.ai, our mission is simple: to put the power of AI and automation in the hands of every business, regardless of size. The same algorithmic precision that drives multi-billion-pound trading floors can — and should — be at work answering your phones, booking your appointments, and growing your revenue.

We are obsessively focused on two outcomes for our customers: efficiency and productivity. Every feature we build is measured against those standards. If it doesn't make your business faster or more profitable, it doesn't ship.

  • Automation-first thinking

    We design every system to remove friction by default — not as an afterthought. Your team should focus on decisions, not administration.

  • Relentless optimisation

    Good enough is never good enough. We tune, iterate, and improve continuously — the same mindset that drives high-frequency trading performance.

  • Human-centred AI

    Our AI doesn't replace your people — it amplifies them. Every interaction is designed to feel natural, warm, and on-brand for your business.

Founder & CEO

Chao Liu

Founder & CEO, Macaws.ai

Chao Liu has spent over 20 years at the cutting edge of technology — first in the high-octane world of investment banking, where he developed high-frequency trading algorithms that executed thousands of decisions per second. Long before "AI" became a buzzword, Chao was building the early quantitative models that would come to define modern algorithmic finance.

A Cambridge University alumnus, Chao brings a rare combination of deep technical rigour and commercial acuity to everything he builds. His academic foundation in mathematics and computer science, forged in one of the world's leading institutions, underpins Macaws.ai's approach to every problem: model it precisely, test it relentlessly, deploy it confidently.

After two decades building systems for institutions managing billions in capital, Chao turned his attention to a different kind of opportunity: the millions of small and medium businesses that are haemorrhaging revenue every day simply because they can't answer every call. Macaws.ai is his answer — bringing institutional-grade AI automation to every business on the planet.

In 2020, Chao's work was recognised with the Queen's Award for Enterprise — Britain's highest business honour — a testament to the impact and innovation at the heart of his work.
